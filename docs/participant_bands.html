Every participant we tested, with their own uncertainty

Each participant's metric is estimated from their own trials and given a bootstrap band. The dataset aggregate is the envelope of those bands — the lowest lower bound and the highest upper bound. It supports one claim: every participant in this test set lands inside it. It is not a population estimate, and no inference to unseen participants is intended.

Aggregate per dataset

The envelope answers “does every participant land in here”. Because it is a max/min statistic it necessarily widens as participants are added — across these datasets its width tracks participant count at r = 0.92. Use the interquartile range when comparing datasets of different size.

Why the envelope and not the mixture

Pooling every participant's bootstrap draws with equal weight gives a narrower interval, shown in the last column. With five participants its 2.5th percentile sits 0.025 × 5 = 0.125 participant-equivalents into the tail — inside the lowest participant's own trial noise. Each endpoint then describes one participant rather than the dataset, which is why the envelope is the headline number here.
